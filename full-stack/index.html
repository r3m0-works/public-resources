Privacy Policy for Full Stack - Demo

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Full Stack - Demo ("the app") is developed by Create & Execute Studios, which acts as the data controller for the limited data described in this policy. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it's used. You can reach us at createnexecute.studios@gmail.com.

Information we collect directly

Full Stack - Demo has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no way to enter personal information. The app stores the following locally, on your device only, and never transmits it anywhere:

  • Your best score
  • Whether sound is muted
  • Whether you've seen the initial instructions

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It is protected by Android's standard per-app sandboxing, which prevents other apps on your device from accessing it. We implement no additional security measures beyond that because this data is never transmitted to us or stored anywhere outside your device.

Information collected by third-party services

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, IP address)
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address, used to serve regionally appropriate ads
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewards granted)
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information

Google uses this data for advertising and marketing and for analytics and performance measurement. Full Stack - Demo shows rewarded video ads only, which you choose to watch; there are no forced or background ads.

This data is collected and processed by Google, governed by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

The app also relies on Google Play Services, which Google provides as part of the Android platform. The privacy policies of the third-party services used by this app are:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and/or device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this app or to other apps and websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your use of this app and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time. On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads and turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization", or reset your advertising ID from the same screen. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising from participating vendors at www.aboutads.info. Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see will be non-personalized.

If you're in the EEA, UK, or another region requiring consent, you'll be shown a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ads load, letting you choose whether to allow personalized advertising.

Because Google's advertising infrastructure operates globally, this data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the United States, under the safeguards Google describes in its Privacy Policy linked above.

Your rights in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, erase, or restrict processing of your personal data, and the right to object to processing. Because Full Stack - Demo holds no account and stores nothing on our servers, the practical ways to exercise these rights are:

  • Withdraw or change consent: open Privacy Settings, available on the title screen (before starting a run), to reopen the consent form and change your advertising choice. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.
  • Reset your identifier: reset your advertising ID in your Android device settings to disconnect past ad data from your device.
  • Erase local data: clearing the app's data, or uninstalling it, removes everything stored on your device.

The lawful basis for processing advertising data in these regions is your consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. For any other request, contact us at createnexecute.studios@gmail.com.

California privacy rights

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, our advertising partner (Google AdMob) collects device identifiers and app usage data to serve personalized ads, which counts as "sharing" under California law. Where available, you can opt out by choosing non-personalized ads in the app's Privacy Settings (title screen, before starting a run), or by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your Android device settings, which always works regardless. We do not knowingly collect or sell the personal information of anyone under 16.

Children's privacy

Full Stack - Demo is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, contact us at createnexecute.studios@gmail.com so we can delete it. Full Stack - Demo does not include an age gate, so we have no way to know a player's age from within the app; if we otherwise learn a specific user is under 13, we will take reasonable steps to ensure only non-personalized ads are served to that user going forward.

Data retention and deletion

Because we don't collect or store any personal data on our own servers, there's nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's own privacy policy and account controls (e.g. resetting your advertising ID, or opting out of ad personalization, in your device's settings).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above, and we encourage you to review this page periodically. Changes to this policy do not alter your advertising consent choices, which remain under your control in Privacy Settings.
